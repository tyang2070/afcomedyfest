**Bren Tran** is a gay, Asian American comedian from Chicago. He's a Bob Curry alum from the Second City and creator of You Win Some, You Dim Sum, a Best Comedy Show '25 Nominee (Chicago Reader) where comics and audiences gather to celebrate their small wins with big laughs. He's performed at comedy festivals all around the world, including Edinburgh Fringe Festival, SF Sketchfest, Alaska B4UDie Festival, RISE Comedy Festival, Madison Comedy Week, and the Big Tomato Comedy Festival. When he's not performing, he's writing scripts and making films of his own - his short film *Jury Duty* was featured in the Atlanta Comedy Film Festival.
