**Josh Fleury** is a half Taiwanese/half Neurodivergent comedy songwriter, editor, performer and musician based in LA. He is best known for his work with Moon Goon, Smosh, Starkid, UCB, Dropout's *Thousandaires*, and his increasingly unhinged TikTok songs. With Moon Goon he's regularly written and performed shows at both UCB and Dynasty Typewriter, including *CityWalk: The Musical*, and *Prime! An Amazon Musical,* dodging lawsuits both times. While editing for Smosh he co-wrote several musical comedy bits across their many channels, series and live shows, most notably for *Try Not To Laugh,* *Anthony is Dead: The Funeral Roast*, and *Sleepover Live!* Earlier this year his band Jacob The Horse made their radio debut on KROQ-FM 106.7 after releasing *At Least It's Almost Over*, their third and angriest album yet.
