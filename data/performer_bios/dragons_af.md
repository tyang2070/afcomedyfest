**Asian AF Presents: DRAGONS AF**

**DRAGONS AF** is the hit comedy TTRPG show with performers from Asian AF! This special one-shot campaign put our heroes once again on a path of revenge with a setting, subclasses, and monsters inspired by Asian lore and myth!

In 2024, Asian AF partnered with comedy podcast network Earwolf to create DRAGONS AF, a Dungeons & Dragons podcast miniseries featuring comedians from Asian AF. In November 2025, Dragons AF returned to the live stage to debut our newest campaign centered around revenge at Outside In Theatre! 

Cast: **Keiko Agena, Jed Alcantara, Alfred Aquino II, Cassi Jerkins, Chuck Maa, Tao Yang** with Special Guest **Zac Oyama** (Dropout TV’s *Dimension 20*)

Dragons AF is produced by **Jed Alcantara, Will Choi, Cassi Jerkins,** & **Chuck Maa**
