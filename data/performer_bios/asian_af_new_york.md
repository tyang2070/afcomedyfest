**Asian AF New York** (@asianafshow) is the New York City chapter of the hit comedy and variety show celebrating AAPI voices. Hosted by resident improv team The Joy Fuck Club, each show brings together the next generation of comedy stars alongside some of its biggest names in stand-up, improv, sketch, storytelling, and beyond for one unforgettable night of comedy.

From razor-sharp stand-up to hilarious character work, heartfelt storytelling, audience surprises, and an improvised finale that somehow always goes gloriously off the rails, Asian AF NY is equal parts showcase, party, and celebration of the incredible AAPI talent shaping New York's comedy scene.

The show has been featured in *The New York Times, NBC News, ABC News, Vulture, The Serica Initiative*, and has headlined major comedy festivals nationwide.

Cast: **Donald Chang, Alex Cheng, Ryan Chittaphong, Kurt Cruz, Florence Friebe, Aakash Kesaverapu, Kendra Singh, Angel Yau**
