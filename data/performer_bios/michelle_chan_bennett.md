**Michelle Chan Bennett** (she/her) is an NYC-based writer and actor whose work has been featured in Vulture, Netflix is a Joke, and The Tonight Show. She is currently the Head of Sketch/Character Programs at the Upright Citizens Brigade, where she also performs regularly. Formerly an Ars Nova resident artist where her musical comedy, *Bada Swing!* premiered off-broadway in May 2023 and continues to sell out around the country. You also may have seen her dancing in *Ziwe’s Pop Show* or starring as “Cheryl” in the BIPOC murder mystery play, *The Art of Killin’ It*.
