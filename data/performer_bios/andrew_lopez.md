As an actor, **Andrew Lopez** stars in AppleTV+ comedy series *Platonic*, opposite Seth Rogen and Rose Byrne, directed and created by Nicholas Stoller and Francesca Delbanco. He appears as Garrett in FX’s *The Bear* part 2, 3, 4, and 6. He will next appear in the Amazon film *Judgement Day* with Will Ferrell and Zac Efron.

As a stand-up comedian, he is a Comedy Cellar regular and was chosen as one of Vulture and New York Magazine’s Comedians You Should and Will Know in 2023. In 2024, he was featured on a segment on *The Today Show* as one of the future faces of comedy. His stand-up is featured on Netflix’s *Jo Koy: In His Elements* and served as a producer. He was the opening act on a worldwide tour opening for Jo Koy performing for thousands of people in arenas and theaters. He has headlined comedy clubs, colleges, and theaters around the world.

In TV, he will write, produce, and star in an untitled show at Netflix. Also another show in development at HBO with Jody Hill directing and Christopher Storer and Roughhouse producing. He is writing and producing *The Finger* at Netflix with David Bernad and Max Hollman producing. Previously he developed at FX with Christopher Storer and Hiro Murai producing and Amazon with Riz Ahmed and Lulu Wang producing. He was the story editor for season 2 of *Unstable* on Netflix. In 2021, he was selected into the Sundance Episodic Lab.  

In features, he was named one of Variety’s Screenwriters to Watch in 2024. He developed a romantic comedy at Amazon. At New Republic Pictures, he is writing an original thriller with director David Michod producing and is developing an action comedy with Macro. He was selected as a 2025 Rideback Rise fellow by Dan Lin and his Rideback board.
