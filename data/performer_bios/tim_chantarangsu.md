**Tim Chantarangsu** (formerly Timothy DeLaGhetto) is an Thai American comedian and rapper who got his start on Youtube with his outlandish sketch comedy and witty rap songs.   
After earning over 4 million Youtube subscribers and over a billion views, he was able to make the jump to television. He was a star cast member on MTV's hiphop improv comedy show *Wild 'N Out* for 8 seasons, while also appearing on shows like Guy Code and Deliciousness. His last studio album Cruise Control helped garner him the first ever number one spot on Billboard's Uncharted chart for independent artists. He just made his sitcom debut on the brand new *Martin* spin off, *The Varnell Hill Show* starring Tommy Davidson.
