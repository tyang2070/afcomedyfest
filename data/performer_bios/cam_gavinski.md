**Cam Gavinski** is a comedian, actor, writer, and animator from Dallas, Texas. Cam was selected as a featured performer for the 2025 *Disney Television Discovers Talent Showcase*. He's a writer at *Smosh* and formerly was a writer for *NBCUniversal* Animation Writer's Program 2023-2024. Cam performs at *UCB* with *Pinot Noir*, *Asssketch*, and previously was on *Betty* and *Maude*. Cam has appeared on *Euphoria* (HBO), *It’s Always Sunny* (FX) and has animated shorts for *Comedy Central*, *The Jim Jefferies Show*, and *NBC*.
