**Zindabaddies**, formerly known as South Asian AF Superteam, is the former house improv team of South Asian AF. Made up of South Asian improvisers, Zindabaddies has performed at every South Asian AF show and also at SF Sketchfest. They are delighted to reunite to celebrate the 10 year anniversary of Asian AF!

Cast: **Anne Akhila, Parvesh Cheena, Shilpa Das, Pallavi Gunalan, Ru Kazi, Dhruv Uday Singh**
