Lily Abha Cratsley & Arasha are the viral South Asian comedy duo behind **How I Met Your Masi** (@howimetyourmasi). Their debut production premiered at Dynasty Typewriter to a sold-out crowd and global livestreaming audience last year, and is currently streaming on demand at Dynasty.tv and in development for a national tour & television pilot. Their festival appearance features a fan-favorite sketch with a fan-favorite guest star, Desi Diva - Suni M!
