**Kausar Mohammed** is a queer, Muslim, South Asian writer and actor. She can be seen as superhero DR. MEENA DHAWAN on CW's *THE FLASH*, to leading on Hulu's horror feature film *APPENDAGE*, and in comedies like *MO, WHAT MEN WANT,* and *LITTLE*. She also voices the beloved lead character, Yaz, in the hit animated *JURASSIC WORLD* shows and as the iconic Cleo in Nickelodeon’s *MONSTER HIGH*.

On stage, Kausar recently produced a sold-out run of GUARDS AT THE TAJ (by Rajiv Joseph), where she starred as the first woman ever to play the leading role of Humayun in its professional production. She’s a founding member of the distinguished all-South Asian sketch comedy troupe, The Get Brown, which performed three seasons of continuously sold-out shows in LA. She is an alumna of Upright Citizens’ Brigade LA's Maude and Betty teams. Her Usha Vance impression recently went viral on social media for its biting commentary. Her comedic work has garnered acclaim in Vogue, Huffington Post, NBC, and more.
