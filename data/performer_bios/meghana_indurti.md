**Meghana Indurti** is a first-generation South Asian screenwriter and stand up comedian who got her start in Chicago and is currently based in LA. She is a humor writer for *The New Yorker* and *Reductress* and a headline contributor for *The Onion.* She wrote and executive produced her comedy short film "Mango Chile Pie", which premiered at CAAM, iSAFF, and NewFest. She is currently developing a dark comedy feature with Shyam Madiraju, was most recently a writer's assistant for Amazon's upcoming show "Bait" from Riz Ahmed, which premiered at Sundance in 2026, and is writing a humor novel about mental health for The Massie & McQuilkin Literary Agency.
