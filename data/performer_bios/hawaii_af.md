**Hawaiʻi AF** (@hawaiiafshow) is a comedy variety show created by and starring Hawai‘i born, based, and connected actors and comedians. Representing the island's melting pot of Asian and Polynesian culture and points of view, this show features improv, sketch comedy, and live music. Two scoops funny wit gravy all ovah. Bring da aunties, bring da uncles, leave da bebehs at home. Go check 'um, go check 'um, GO\!
