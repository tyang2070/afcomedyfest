**Sundeep Morrison** (they/them) is an award-winning queer, non-binary, Panjabi Sikh, Canadian actor, writer, and director. Recently, Sundeep starred in the short film *FLIGHT 182,* written and directed by Rippin Sindher, and executive-produced by Ridley Scott and Archie Panjabi. Sundeep received the Best Drama award at United Solo in New York for their solo show, *RAG HEAD: An* *American Story.* A graduate of the American Musical and Dramatic Academy in New York, they focus their storytelling on social justice, sexuality, and gender.
