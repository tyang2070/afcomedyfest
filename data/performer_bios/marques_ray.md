**Marques Ray** is an actor, comedian, and interpretive dancer. You may have seen him on hit shows like *Curb Your Enthusiasm*, *The Good Place*, or *Arrested Development* but his true passion is “dancing for the wind.” He’s performed, written, and taught sketch and improv comedy across the globe and frequently exercises his unique character work at local theaters. You can catch him and his friends now in his completely improvised web-series, *MISREPRESENTED*.
