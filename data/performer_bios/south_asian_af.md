***South Asian AF*** **(@southasianaf)** is a comedy variety show featuring South Asian comedians and improvisers doing sketches, improv, characters, and more! The show enjoyed a 6 year sold out run starting in 2018 and they are delighted to return for a reunion show to celebrate the 10 year anniversary of Asian AF! Bringing together an all star cast featuring veteran show performers along with some new faces, South Asian AF is back for one special night!!!
