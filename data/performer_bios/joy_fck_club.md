The Joy F*ck Club is an NYC-based all-AAPI long-form improv comedy ensemble best known as the house team of *Asian AF NY*, the acclaimed monthly show at the Upright Citizens Brigade Theatre in NYC. Bringing together a powerhouse mix of performers, writers, and improvisers from the Upright Citizens Brigade, The Magnet Theater, Brooklyn Comedy Collective, and beyond, the team has become a staple of New York City's comedy scene.

Known for their high-energy, fast-paced performances, The Joy F*ck Club transforms personal stories into wildly imaginative worlds, blending cultural specificity with absurdity, chaos, and sharp comedic instincts. The name is a loving, inappropriate homage to the celebrated novel and film *The Joy Luck Club*, which tells you pretty much everything you need to know about the team's comedic sensibilities.

The Joy F*ck Club has headlined and performed at comedy festivals and venues across the country, including The Kennedy Center, the New York Comedy Festival, Asian Comedy Fest, the Del Close Marathon, Hartford Improv Festival, Fun and Dumb Improv Festival, and many more.
