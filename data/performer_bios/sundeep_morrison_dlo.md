**Sundeep Morrison** and **D'Lo**, your favorite queer storytellers couldn't be here today, so they sent their aunties instead. 

Soon to be your favorites, Chandra Aunty and Gulabo Aunty, are showing up as who they are: the Aunties Who Love You.   
These aunties have lifted the rug on their own existence via therapy, fierce karaoke battles, long digestive walks, and their annual mushroom trips. They are not your typical Aunties. This is why they have so much love to give and less damns to give.    
Come get loved up on by them, or join them in doing a little dance, making a little noise.  
Without further ado: the Aunties Who Love You!
