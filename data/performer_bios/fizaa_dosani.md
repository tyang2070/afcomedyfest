**Fizaa Dosani** is a comedian and satirist who was forged in the heat of the Florida sun. She has millions of views across social media and performs on stages and festivals across the country, including Netflix is a Joke, SF Sketchfest, and New York Comedy Festival. As an actor, Fizaa has had roles in popular television shows like CBS’s *S.W.AT.*, Netflix’s *Lincoln Lawyer*, and FOX’s *Pivoting*. In response to tokenism in arts and media, Fizaa created Facial Recognition Comedy to showcase the vast diversity of comics within her demographic. The stand-up show has been mentioned in the New York Times, Deadline, Vulture, Backstage, and more. Currently, Fizaa co-produces and hosts Facial Recognition Comedy every month at The World Famous Comedy Store in Los Angeles. She is also a devoted mother to two huskies and ten succulents.
