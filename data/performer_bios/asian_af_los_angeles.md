Established in 2016, **Asian AF** (@asianafshow) quickly cemented its status as a must-see comedy show in Los Angeles, New York, and beyond — spotlighting the hottest rising stars and powerhouse Asian American talent in improv, stand-up, sketch, characters, musical comedy, storytelling, and more. Asian AF celebrates its 10-year anniversary by putting on the AF Comedy Festival!

Asian AF has headlined major comedy festivals, including SF Sketchfest, the New York Comedy Festival, Clusterfest, the Del Close Marathon, the Hawaii Comedy Festival, Richmond’s 2nd Best Fest, Berlin’s Das Improv Festival, London’s Big Scene Improv Festival, the Edinburgh International Improv Festival, the Kennedy Center in Washington, D.C., and many more.
