**Kunal Dudheker** is a South Asian actor, comedian, and writer from Orange County, CA. Some of his television credits include *Hacks* (HBO), *No Good Deed* (Netflix), *Crazy Ex-Girlfriend* (CW), and recurring roles on *Silicon Valley* (HBO), *Better Things* (FX), and *Life by Ella* (Apple TV). Voice-over credits include Pixar’s *Dream Productions*, *The Flash: Escape the Midnight Circus*, and *The Really Loud House*. On film, Kunal was featured in Marvel Studios’ *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, Ad Astra* and more. His short film *Group C*, which he wrote and starred in, won Best Comedy at the Cannes Indie Shorts Awards. He has also performed regularly at the Upright Citizens Brigade, various comedy venues in CA and NY, and the most prestigious of all, the internet. In his free time, he enjoys jumping in rivers and oceans, and playing with other people’s dogs.
