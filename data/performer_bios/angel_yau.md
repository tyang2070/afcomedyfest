**Angel Yau** is a New York City–based comedian, storyteller, filmmaker, and producer. She is one of the producers for NYC Asian AF and performs with Joy Fuck Club. She also started and produces Asian American Film Thing. She was UCB's Maude, Characters, and its (short-lived) Digital Teams. Angel is also an award-winning stop-motion filmmaker whose films transform random childhood memories into heartfelt, comedic stories. She has been featured on HBO, Netflix Is a Joke, and the BBC. She once gave a TEDx talk about peeing.
