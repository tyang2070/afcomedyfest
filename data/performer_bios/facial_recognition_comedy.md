**Facial Recognition Comedy** (FRC) is a nationally touring stand-up show featuring female-identifying performers of MENASA (Middle Eastern, North African, & South Asian) descent. The show was created as a response to tokenism and underrepresentation in entertainment, and to let everyone know that we are NOT the same person!  
   
FRC has had bicoastal residencies in NYC (New York Comedy Club) and LA (The World Famous Comedy Store), annual appearances at SF Sketchfest, and was listed by The New York Times as one of the top 5 shows they were most excited to see at the NYC Comedy Festival. FRC has also been a Creators First Clubhouse show, and has also been mentioned in Deadline, Daily Beast, Vulture, Screen Rant, Backstage, and more.  
   
Facial Recognition Comedy is executive-produced by comedians Fizaa Dosani, Zahra Ali, and Pallavi Gunalan.
