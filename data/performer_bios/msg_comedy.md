**M.S.G. Comedy**, formerly No MSG Added Comedy, is a comedy collective founded in 2018\. Inspired by shows like *SNL* and *MadTV*, the collective focuses on connecting community through humor and laughter. Since its founding, M.S.G. Comedy has performed all over California at events that include Coup De Comedy Festival, SF Sketch Fest, The Marsh Studio, and Chance Theater.  The team is made up of writers and performers that have stage credits at South Coast Rep, the Second City Mainstage, Long Beach Playhouse, as well as film/tv credits on *Jury Duty Presents, Spidey & His Amazing Friends, Last Comic Standing,* and *PEN15*.
