**Shilpa Das** is a writer/actor/comedian based in LA. She has been a writer and actor on UCB's long running scripted musical comedy company, Quick & Funny Musicals since 2018. Shilpa has performed musical improv for many years as a member of teams including rap/musical group Kaboombox, UCB mess hall team, Almost Midnight, current running group, Sweet Sweet Fantasy, and as a guest performer with Baby Wants Candy in Shamilton. She's also appeared in various comedy musicals in LA including Bachelor the Musical, Tuckcracker, Heated Rivalry: The Musical Parody! and . She is also known for her work as the producer/co-host of the South Asian AF show series.
