**Tatyana Komaguchi a.k.a "GUCHI"** is a standup comedian blending sharp storytelling with punchline-driven humor rooted in Black, Queer, and Asian perspectives.

Tatyana's talent has garnered recognition, including participation in Sketch Fest '23 & '24, being named a Laugh Factory Fresh Face, as a Finalist in the ABFF Because They're Funny Comedy Festival's Breakout Comedian Of The Year, on Nick Cannon's *The Daily Cannon*, and one of the new friends of SMOSH.
