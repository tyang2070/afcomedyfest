**Sam Oh** is a writer and comedian from Los Angeles (well technically The Valley which, like, totally counts!) You can see him starring opposite Jennifer Garner and John Cena in *One Attempt Remaining* directed by Kay Cannon, coming soon to Netflix.

As a queer child of Korean Christian immigrants, Oh had no choice but to develop a sense of humor. He got his start as a teenager doing open-mics all over LA and writing on UCB’s Maude Night. An alum of the WarnerMedia Access Writers Program and NBC's Late Night Writers Workshop, his scripts have placed in numerous industry competitions including the Top 50 of the Academy Nicholl Fellowship.

As a comic, he’s been featured in both The LA Times and The New York Times—a true bicoastal baddie. He’s opened for ALOK as part of Netflix Is A Joke Festival and co-headlined Joe’s Pub NYC. He recently debuted his solo show, *The Unbearable Tightness of Being… Gay Virgin*, which sold out Dynasty Typewriter.

Oh performs comedic music as his hyper-sexual pop star alter ego, Gay Virgin. The Gay Virgin EP is available on all streaming platforms. You can watch the official music video for his viral song 4SKIN on YouTube, which spawned the 4SKIN Cho Remix featuring Margaret Cho. 

He is currently developing a feature starring and executive produced by Margaret Cho, as well as hosting a tarot talk show produced by Linden Entertainment. For updates follow @gayvirginofficial.
