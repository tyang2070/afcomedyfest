**Writing and Producing a Short Film with Joy Regullano (Writing Workshop)**

**Workshop Description**: So you’ve got an idea for a short film. Or an idea for a feature that you want to shoot as a proof of concept. Or maybe you don’t have any ideas yet, but you’re tired of waiting around for someone else to cast you, staff you as a writer, and/or hire you as a director, and everyone keeps telling you to make your own shit. Or maybe you've made a short and want to learn about cool festivals that offer money for you to screen your film and attend the fest. 

This 2 hour workshop will cover turning your idea into a reality - how to go from conception to screening at film festivals. We’ll watch a few shorts, discuss short film grants you can apply for as well as film festival and crowdfunding strategies, and workshop your ideas. You’ll walk out with a plan for next steps, a list of film festivals, and be inspired creatively to take your career into your own hands. 

**Optional**: Bring in 1 - 3 short film ideas you’d like to workshop and something to take notes with (pen/paper, laptop, etc).

**Instructor Info**: Joy Regullano is a writer / actor / comedian based in LA who wrote on the animated show MONSTERS AT WORK (based on MONSTERS INC) on Disney Plus. Previously, she wrote on JELLY, BEN, and POGO (a PBS Kids web series), KUNG FU PANDA: DRAGON KNIGHT (Netflix / Dreamworks), HEALING POWERS OF DUDE (Netflix), several Sesame Workshop digital shorts, and she was a Sesame Street writing fellow. TV acting credits include BARRY, LINCOLN LAWYER, CRAZY EX-GIRLFRIEND, MODERN FAMILY, SPEECHLESS, and SUPERNATURAL. She has performed improv and sketch around LA at UCB and Second City Hollywood. Her YouTube sketch WHITE FETISH went viral (1M+ views) and was featured in the Huffington Post, Upworthy, and more. She was recently nominated for an Emmy for writing on Sesame Street’s Word of the Day series. She has taught film writing, sketch writing, and pilot writing for Second City Hollywood as well as sketch writing for Improv Hawaii. She has also taught improv, writing, and acting workshops for Pacific Symphony’s summer camp Arts-X-Press, Canyon Kids Film Lab, and the Glendale Adventist girls volleyball team. She won a Birns & Sawyer / Fil Am Creative Film Festival camera grant, and her short films Te Seguiré a la Oscuridad, I Won’t Miss You, and White Fetish have played in dozens of festivals over the years and won multiple awards.
