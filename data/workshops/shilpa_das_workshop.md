**Write A Comedy Song 101 with Shilpa Das (Musical Comedy Workshop)**

**Workshop Description**: Learn how to write a musical comedy song from A to Z. In the class, I will share my tried and true methods for writing a musical comedy song. Together, we will choose a topic, go over outlining and format and write a full musical comedy song together! Musical experience is welcome, but not necessary! 

**Note**: *We will not be going over how to play an instrument but you are welcome to bring one if you'd like to play along during the workshop!*

**Date/Time:** Sunday, September 6th @ 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM (2 hours)

**Price:** $40

**Max Attendees**: 10

**Prerequisite**: N/A

**Instructor Info**: Shilpa Das is a writer/actor/comedian based in LA. She has been a writer and actor on UCB's long running scripted musical comedy company, Quick & Funny Musicals since 2018. Shilpa has performed musical improv for many years as a member of teams including rap/musical group Kaboombox, UCB mess hall team, Almost Midnight, current running group, Sweet Sweet Fantasy, and as a guest performer with Baby Wants Candy in Shamilton. She's also appeared in various comedy musicals in LA including Bachelor the Musical, Tuckcracker, Heated Rivalry: The Musical Parody! and . She is also known for her work as the producer/co-host of the South Asian AF show series.
