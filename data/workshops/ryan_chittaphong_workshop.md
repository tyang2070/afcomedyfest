**Stella Adler's Acting Technique for Sketch & Improv with Ryan Chittaphong (Acting Workshop)**

**Workshop Description**: Led by Ryan Chittaphong, Director of Artistic & Student Engagement at the Stella Adler Studio of Acting (and UCB regular), participants will learn the foundation of Stella Adler's teachings and the acting techniques utilized by many artists in the film, television and theater industries. In this hands-on seminar participants explore actions, circumstance and justification; and make courageous, expansive onstage choices using their limitless imaginations as their primary resource.

**Date/Time:** Friday, September 4th @ 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM (2 hours)

**Price:** $40

**Max Attendees**: 16

**Prerequisite**: N/A

**Instructor Info**: Ryan Chittaphong has been with the Stella Adler Studio since 2008 where in addition to serving as Director of Artistic & Student Engagement, he teaches Acting Technique, Character and Improvisation. Ryan also oversees the marketing and recruitment departments of the studio.  At the Stella Adler Studio he has been mentored in the technique by Master Teachers Ron Burrus, Antonio Merenda, Betsy Parrish and Artistic Director Tom Oppenheim. He studied improvisation and sketch comedy at the Upright Citizens Brigade and Magnet Theater. Ryan frequently performs improv comedy in New York on house teams at Upright Citizens Brigade, The Armory Comedy, and in the hit monthly Ten Bones Theatre show “Entirely From Memory” at Littlefield. He is currently co-producer of the popular Asian comedy variety show “ASIAN AF” at UCB. He also performs with the Magnet Theater, People’s Improv Theater, Brooklyn Comedy Collective, Second City New York, and other indie groups.
