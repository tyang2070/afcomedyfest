**Get Out of Your Head And Into Your Feelings! with Lilan Bowden (Improv Workshop)**

**Workshop Description**: A cure to over-complicated heady improv scenes: Check in with a feeling! This workshop focuses on harnessing emotion to provide hardy engines for your improv scenes. We’ll explore how this approach makes scenes easy, and I share why I think it’s the best!

**Date/Time:** Saturday, September 5th @ 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM (2 hours)

**Price:** $40

**Max Attendees**: 16

**Prerequisite**: N/A – Improv students of all levels welcome

**Instructor Info**: Lilan Bowden is an actress and director based out of Los Angeles. She plays “Tia” on Apple TV’s Emmy Award winning show SHRINKING, “Robin Knight” on NCIS, “Amber Kang” on Netflix’s MURDERVILLE, and has had many other notable roles in television shows such as PARKS AND RECREATION, LOVE, THE PETE HOLMES SHOW, along with her breakout role “Bex” in Disney Channel’s first TV show to feature an Asian American family, ANDI MACK (Peabody/BAFTA nominated/Honors Academy Award winner). She is an alum of the Disney Entertainment Television (DET) Directing Program, where she directed the multi-cam comedy VILLIANS OF VALLEY VIEW. Her directorial debut film BECOMING EDDIE was an Official Selection at film festivals across the country and inspired the collaboration with Sony Pictures Entertainment for TV show development.

She has performed live comedy at the Los Angeles Upright Citizens Brigade Theater for over 10 years with improv team Rococo and sketch team Bonafide, and with shows she co-produced, Lilan and Wilder’s Best Day Ever (sketch comedy). She continues to perform in various improv shows and, of course, the long running variety comedy show Asian AF.
