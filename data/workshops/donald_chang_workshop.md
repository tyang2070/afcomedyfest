**Improvised Blockbuster with Donald Chang (Improv Workshop)**

**Workshop Description**: Go full Hollywood and learn the improvised movie format! Lovers will reunite in the rain, loose cannon detectives will walk away from explosions, and wizards will battle kaiju - all in your scenes!

**Date/Time:** Friday, September 4th @ 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM (2 hours)

**Price:** $40

**Max Attendees**: 16

**Prerequisite**: At least 1 year of improv experience

**Instructor Info**: Donald Chang is a NYC based, Korean actor, VA, comedian and writer. You can see him on HBO, CBS, Netflix and Disney+. You can hear his voice on TV, video games, audio books, and even the Calm app. He regularly improvises on ASSSScat and the weekend team The Prophecy, and performs characters on Cabal.
