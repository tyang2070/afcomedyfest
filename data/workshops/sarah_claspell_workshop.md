**Callbacks & Connections with Sarah Claspell (Improv Workshop)**

**Workshop Description**: A good callback or connection can bring down the house, or help you blow out your game in new, fun ways. We’ll work on callbacks & connections, game, point of view, second beats, all that good stuff to level up your improv.

**Date/Time:** Thursday, September 3rd @ 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM (2 hours)

**Price:** $40

**Max Attendees**: 16

**Prerequisite**: At least 1 year of improv experience

**Instructor Info**: Sarah Claspell is an actor/comedian/writer living in Los Angeles. She co-owns and teaches at World’s Greatest Improv School, teaches at the Upright Citizens Brigade, and performs regularly with improv teams The Bozos, Cardinal Redbird, Voltron, Living Room Living Room, and co-hosts the variety show Asian AF. She has performed at SF Sketchfest, Das Improv Festival, The Big Scene in London, Vietnam Improv Festival, Edinburgh Fringe and Edinburgh International Improv Festival. She has been featured on tv shows such as Brooklyn Nine-Nine, Make Some Noise on Dropout, and others. Sarah also co-hosts the podcast Everything is Rent, a podcast about the Broadway musical Rent.
